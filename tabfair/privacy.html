Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 18, 2026

Overview

Tabfair (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Tabfair does not collect personal information. The app works entirely on your device and does not require accounts, logins, or data transmission to external servers.

Data Storage

All calculations and preferences are processed locally on your device. We do not store, transmit, or access bill amounts, tip values, or split configurations.

Contacts Access (Optional)

Tabfair optionally allows importing contact display names to label splits.

  • Requires explicit user permission
  • Reads display names only — no phone numbers or emails
  • Processed entirely on-device
  • Not stored, transmitted, or shared
  • Permission can be revoked at any time

Third-Party Services

Tabfair does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children’s Privacy

Tabfair does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the app store listing.
