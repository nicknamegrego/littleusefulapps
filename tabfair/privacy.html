Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 18, 2026

Overview

Tabfair ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our bill splitting application.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Tabfair is designed to work entirely on your device without requiring any account creation, login, or data transmission to external servers.

Data Storage

All calculations and preferences are processed locally on your device. We do not store, transmit, or have access to any bill amounts, tip calculations, or split configurations you enter into the app.

Contacts Access (Optional)

Tabfair offers an optional feature to import names from your device contacts to personalize bill splits. This feature:

  • Requires your explicit permission before accessing contacts
  • Only reads display names — no phone numbers or emails
  • Processes all data locally on your device
  • Does not store, transmit, or share contact information
  • Never modifies contacts
  • Can be revoked at any time through device settings

Third-Party Services

Tabfair does not integrate with analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children’s Privacy

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions, please contact us through the app store listing.
